Taking it to the Model:Cycles

The 6 rows match the Cycles' 6 tracks. The machine to load is under each row name, and the 16 steps match its 16 trig keys.

Swing is shown as an Elektron-style percentage (50% = straight, ~67% = triplet shuffle), so you can type the same number into the Cycles.

The kit line under each genre describes the sound to aim for (kick pitch and decay, rim vs. clap, how dark). Use it as a starting point for tweaking the Cycles' machines.

Soft hits → put a lower volume/velocity parameter lock on that trig. Rolls → use the Cycles' retrig or a short repeat at 1/32.

How electronic tracks are built

Club tracks rarely have verses and choruses. They are built from energy sections that add and take away parts, always in blocks of 8, 16 or 32 bars. On this app and on the Cycles, 1 bar = 16 steps = one page; a pattern holds up to 4 pages.

SongwritingElectronicWhat happens
IntroDJ introDrums only, entering one by one, so a DJ can mix in
VerseGrooveThe full rhythm, but the top layer is held back
Pre-chorusBuild-upKick out, snare roll gets denser, tension rises
ChorusDrop / mainEverything at once: the payoff
BridgeBreakdownKick and bass removed, chords get the space
OutroDJ outroMirror of the intro: parts leave, drums stay last

Rap beats, synthwave and reggaeton keep the song form (verse / hook) because they carry a vocal.

Count in phrases

4 bars
The smallest unit. A loop that repeats 4 times feels complete.
8 bars
One phrase. Small changes happen here: a new hat, a fill.
16–32 bars
One section. Big changes happen here: kick out, drop.
Where
Changes land on bar 9, 17, 33, 65: the first bar of a new block. Never on bar 7 or 13.
Signal
A fill in the last bar of a block tells the listener something is about to change.

Tension by removal

Taking a part away is as strong as adding one. The drop works because the kick was missing.

  1. Kick out. The strongest move. 8–32 bars without kick make its return feel huge. [FUNC] + [T1]
  2. Bass out, kick stays. The track gets light and thin. Good for the last 8 bars of a groove section. [FUNC] + [T5]
  3. Clap out in the groove. Save the backbeat for the drop so it has something new. [FUNC] + [T2]
  4. Down to one element. Just hats or percussion for 4–8 bars before the build.
  5. One beat of silence. Mute everything on the last beat before the drop. The gap makes the hit land.

On the Model:Cycles

Live
Play one pattern and arrange with mutes: [FUNC] + [T1–6]. Mutes stay when you change patterns.
Fills
Hold a trig in grid recording and turn CHANCE to set the condition FILL. Those trigs only play while you hold [PAGE]. [TRACK] + [PAGE] latches it.
Sections
One pattern per section: copy A01 to A02 ([FUNC] + [RECORD], then [FUNC] + [STOP]), then clear the tracks the section does not use.
Chain
Hold [PATTERN], press the trig keys in order (press one twice to repeat it). Up to 64 patterns; the chain loops.

Effects

Shared reverb & delay

Like on the Model:Cycles, every track sends into one shared reverb and one shared delay. These set how that space sounds.
